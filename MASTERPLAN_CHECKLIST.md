## Chapter-level rules and flags

- Words never enter a perspective transform; one readout position per scene — **[IMPLEMENTED]** (the readout is the only place words live; station shapes carry no text).
- Recipe rule (stages and outcomes, never mechanisms; no tool or provider names) — **[IMPLEMENTED]** across stations, diagrams and the assistant.
- Published names, prices and scope lines verbatim — **[IMPLEMENTED]**; the assistant's knowledge is copied from the pages.
- Arabic leads; numerals 0-9 everywhere; the shadda on صبّارة — **[IMPLEMENTED]** and scanned.
- Smart Portfolio price on the site (499 / 249) versus the master context (1,900 / 890) — **flagged, not changed** (open question 17; the site's published price is kept).
- The hero's terminating full stop — **flagged, not changed** (a copy decision for the founders).
- Site instrumentation (track.js / lead.js) — **out of scope** for this build (open question 7).
